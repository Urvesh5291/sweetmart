# Patel Sweet Mart --- Premium Website Image Generation Guide

## Visual Art Direction, Prompt System & Production Standards

**Brand:** પટેલ સ્વીટ માર્ટ / Patel Sweet Mart\
**Established:** 1995\
**Purpose:** Website imagery, e-commerce photography, campaigns, social
crops, banners, category imagery and AI-assisted visual production\
**Version:** 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Visual North Star

Patel Sweet Mart imagery must feel like a **premium contemporary
Gujarati food house**, not a generic mithai shop.

Every image should combine:

**Authentic Indian food + Gujarati warmth + modern editorial
photography + restrained luxury + appetite appeal**

The desired reaction is:

> "This feels unmistakably Indian, beautifully premium, fresh,
> trustworthy and made for today."

The food must always remain the hero.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Brand Color Environment

Use the approved brand palette as the visual environment:

  Role             Color           HEX
  ---------------- --------------- -----------
  Primary          Royal Cobalt    `#173F8A`
  Energy           Kesari Orange   `#F47B20`
  Festive Accent   Rose            `#CE496B`
  Warm Canvas      Almond          `#F5E6CB`
  Neutral          Charcoal        `#242424`
  Luxury Detail    Heritage Gold   `#D4AF37`

## Image Color Rule

Do **not** force all six colors into every image.

Preferred photographic balance:

-   Almond / warm neutral surfaces: dominant
-   Natural food colors: dominant
-   Royal Cobalt: recognizable brand prop/background accent
-   Kesari: energetic secondary accent
-   Rose: occasional festive/floral accent
-   Gold: very limited premium detail
-   Charcoal: contrast and depth

Food color must remain believable. Never recolor sweets unnaturally to
match the brand palette.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Core Photography Style

## Master Look

**Premium editorial food photography with authentic Indian warmth.**

Characteristics:

-   Photorealistic
-   High-end commercial food photography
-   Rich micro-texture
-   Natural ingredient detail
-   Controlled warm light
-   Soft directional shadows
-   Refined highlights
-   Shallow-to-medium depth of field
-   Sophisticated composition
-   Clean surfaces
-   Minimal visual clutter
-   Realistic portions
-   True-to-life food texture
-   Premium but welcoming

Avoid an artificial CGI appearance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Lighting Direction

Preferred lighting:

-   Large soft directional key light
-   Warm daylight or premium studio daylight
-   Gentle side/back light for texture
-   Controlled highlights on ghee, nuts and silver leaf
-   Soft shadow falloff
-   Warm but neutral white balance

Avoid:

-   Extreme orange/yellow cast
-   Neon lighting
-   Harsh flash
-   Crushed black shadows
-   Excessive HDR
-   Unrealistic glowing food
-   Heavy lens flares
-   Fake metallic reflections everywhere

The image should feel expensive because of **light quality and
composition**, not effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Surface & Material Library

Preferred surfaces:

-   Warm ivory stone
-   Cream marble
-   Light travertine
-   Warm natural wood
-   Matte almond plaster
-   Fine cotton/linen
-   Subtle handcrafted ceramic
-   Brushed brass in tiny amounts
-   Premium cobalt packaging
-   Rose/kesari paper accents

Use traditional Indian brass/silver serveware selectively.

Do not make every image look like a royal wedding set.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Food Styling Standards

## Sweets

Show:

-   Accurate shapes
-   Visible nut texture
-   Realistic ghee sheen
-   Controlled silver leaf where authentic
-   Pistachio/almond garnish used naturally
-   Fresh edges and crumb
-   Premium arrangement
-   A few deliberate imperfections for realism

Examples:

-   Kaju katli
-   Motichoor ladoo
-   Mohanthal
-   Peda
-   Barfi
-   Dry-fruit sweets
-   Besan sweets
-   Seasonal/festival sweets

## Namkeen / Farsan

Show:

-   Crisp texture
-   Individual strands/pieces clearly visible
-   Natural spice variation
-   Fresh fried texture without excessive grease
-   Generous but controlled serving
-   Authentic bowls/trays

Examples:

-   Sev
-   Gathiya
-   Bhavnagari mix
-   Chivda
-   Farsan mixes
-   Dry snacks

# 7. Website Image Families

The website should not use one photography composition everywhere.

Create these consistent image families.

## A. Cinematic Hero Images

**Purpose:** Homepage and major campaigns.

Style:

-   Wide editorial composition
-   One strong food story
-   Generous negative space for live Gujarati headline
-   Premium table/surface
-   Brand colors integrated through props
-   Dramatic but natural lighting
-   No text generated inside image

Recommended master ratio:

**16:9**

Generate larger than final display resolution.

### Composition

For Gujarati headline on left: - Keep left 35--45% relatively clean -
Place hero product toward center-right/right

For headline on right: - Mirror composition

Never generate the actual website headline inside the photograph.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## B. Category Images

**Purpose:** Mithai, Namkeen, Gifting, Dry Fruits, Bakery/Future
categories.

Recommended ratios:

**4:5 or 1:1**

Style:

-   Strong single-category identity
-   3--7 representative products
-   Clean Almond or contextual background
-   Consistent camera angle across categories
-   Minimal props

All category images should look like part of one shoot.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## C. Product Listing Images

**Purpose:** Product cards.

Recommended ratio:

**1:1**

Requirements:

-   Product centered
-   Consistent scale
-   Consistent camera height
-   Soft neutral background
-   Minimal shadows
-   No decorative text
-   No fake packaging claims
-   Accurate product appearance

The user must be able to compare products easily.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## D. Product Detail Hero

**Purpose:** PDP main gallery.

Recommended ratios:

**4:5 + 1:1 supporting crops**

Include a sequence:

1.  Clean hero product
2.  Macro texture
3.  Serving/context shot
4.  Ingredient/detail shot
5.  Packaging shot when applicable

Do not use five near-identical angles.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## E. Heritage / Story Images

**Purpose:** "Since 1995", About, craft story.

Style:

-   Warm documentary/editorial
-   Hands preparing or serving food where appropriate
-   Ingredient craftsmanship
-   Shop/production details
-   Natural gestures
-   No staged corporate handshake imagery

When representing real historical facts, do not fabricate fake archival
photographs as factual evidence. Clearly treat recreated imagery as
illustrative if used.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## F. Premium Gifting Images

Style:

-   Cobalt gift boxes
-   Almond/ivory surfaces
-   Restrained gold detailing
-   Kesari/Rose secondary accents
-   Beautiful opening/unboxing composition
-   Sweets arranged precisely
-   Premium ribbon/paper only when appropriate
-   Festival warmth without excessive decoration

Ratios:

**4:5, 1:1, 16:9**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## G. Festival Campaign Images

Festival campaigns may become more expressive.

Use:

-   Kesari
-   Rose
-   Cobalt
-   restrained Gold
-   diya/floral/textile cues where contextually appropriate

Still maintain negative space and premium art direction.

Avoid visual cliché overload.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Human Presence

People are optional.

When used, prefer:

-   Hands serving sweets
-   Family sharing moments
-   Gift exchange
-   Craft/process gestures
-   Natural Indian/Gujarati context

The image should feel candid and refined.

Avoid:

-   Generic stock-photo smiles
-   Everyone looking at camera
-   Overly posed families
-   Artificial luxury costumes unless campaign-specific
-   Hands with anatomical errors

For product and hero photography, hands-only compositions are often more
timeless.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Composition System

## Rule of Focus

Each image needs one clear visual hierarchy:

**Hero food → supporting food/prop → environment**

Do not give every object equal emphasis.

## Negative Space

Negative space is mandatory for many website images.

Allow clean space for:

-   Gujarati headings
-   CTA buttons
-   Breadcrumbs
-   Campaign copy

Do not ask the image generator to render the final UI or typography into
the image.

## Depth

Use:

-   Foreground detail sparingly
-   Sharp hero subject
-   Soft background separation

Avoid excessive blur that makes the food look miniature or artificial.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Camera Language

Preferred visual language:

### Hero

-   35--50mm equivalent
-   Eye-level to slightly elevated
-   Editorial environment

### Product

-   50--85mm equivalent
-   Controlled perspective
-   Minimal distortion

### Macro

-   85--100mm macro feeling
-   Detailed nut, crumb, syrup, ghee and spice texture

### Flat Lay

Use selectively for: - Gift collections - Festival assortments -
Category discovery

Do not make every image a flat lay.

# 21. Logo & Packaging Accuracy

AI image generation should **not be trusted to reproduce the official
logo or Gujarati wordmark accurately**.

Preferred workflow:

1.  Generate the photography without brand text.
2.  Use a clean placeholder/unbranded package surface where necessary.
3.  Apply the approved vector Patel Sweet Mart logo during
    design/compositing.
4.  Add real Gujarati copy in HTML/UI or professional design software.
5.  Verify every packaging claim manually.

Never publish hallucinated Gujarati text generated inside an image.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Product Accuracy Rule

For real products sold by Patel Sweet Mart, the final e-commerce catalog
should preferably use **real photography of the actual product**.

AI imagery is strongest for:

-   Hero campaigns
-   Art direction concepts
-   Lifestyle scenes
-   Background environments
-   Festival campaigns
-   Editorial storytelling
-   Pre-production visualization

Do not use AI to materially misrepresent:

-   Product quantity
-   Size
-   Ingredients
-   Packaging contents
-   Color
-   Garnish
-   Included accessories
-   Gift-box configuration

The customer should receive what the website visually represents.

# 23. Website Crop Strategy

Never generate only one crop for a major campaign.

For each hero concept create:

``` text
Desktop Hero       16:9
Wide Banner        21:9 where needed
Tablet             4:3
Mobile Hero        4:5
Social Square      1:1
Story/Reel         9:16
```

Do not simply crop a desktop image into mobile if the subject becomes
unusable.

Generate/recompose a mobile-safe version while preserving the same
visual story.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Safe Zones

For hero imagery:

``` text
Text-safe zone: 35–45%
Food/subject zone: 45–60%
Outer crop protection: ~8–10%
```

Important food and packaging should never touch the extreme edge.

The mobile safe zone must be reviewed separately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 25. Image Resolution & Delivery

Generate at the highest practical source quality.

For website delivery:

-   Preserve a high-resolution master
-   Export responsive derivatives
-   Prefer AVIF/WebP where supported
-   Use JPEG only where appropriate
-   Avoid PNG for full photographic images unless transparency is
    required
-   Compress visually, not blindly
-   Use responsive `srcset`
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold images
-   Define image dimensions to prevent layout shift

Do not upload the raw largest AI output directly to every page.

# 27. Alt Text

Alt text describes the meaningful visual content, not the art direction.

Good:

``` text
Assorted kaju katli, ladoo and peda arranged in a serving bowl.
```

Do not write:

``` text
Beautiful premium cinematic 8K luxury Patel Sweet Mart image.
```

Decorative images may use empty alt text when appropriate.

Localize meaningful alt text for Gujarati and English experiences.

-   Dark maroon-and-gold generic mithai branding
-   Palace interiors as the default setting
-   Excessive diya/rangoli imagery on normal pages
-   Rainbow-colored backgrounds
-   Fake royal luxury
-   Heavy gold embossing everywhere
-   Hyper-saturated orange food
-   Floating sweets
-   Impossible ingredient explosions
-   Generic white-background stock photos mixed with cinematic imagery
-   Cartoon food for primary product presentation
-   AI-generated Gujarati text

-   Fake awards or certification marks
-   Unverified heritage imagery

# 31. Image Generation Workflow

For every major campaign:

``` text
1. Define page purpose
2. Define product/food subject
3. Choose image family
4. Choose crop/aspect ratio
5. Define text-safe area
6. Apply brand palette subtly
7. Generate 3–6 composition candidates
8. Reject inaccurate food immediately
9. Select strongest composition
10. Refine lighting/material realism
11. Generate mobile-specific composition
12. Add official logo/copy outside AI generation
13. Optimize for web
14. Add localized alt text
15. QA against brand checklist
```

Consistency matters more than producing many unrelated images.

# 32. Antigravity Implementation Note

Antigravity should treat images as **content assets**, not backgrounds
that contain important copy.

Website typography, Gujarati copy, CTA labels, prices and product
information must remain live HTML wherever possible.

Design components must support:

-   Desktop and mobile art direction
-   `<picture>` sources where appropriate
-   Responsive images
-   Lazy loading
-   Localized alt text
-   Fixed aspect-ratio containers
-   Object-position controls
-   Optional focal-point metadata

Recommended asset metadata model:

``` json
{
  "id": "home-hero-01",
  "purpose": "homepage-hero",
  "desktop": "/images/home/hero-01-16x9.avif",
  "mobile": "/images/home/hero-01-4x5.avif",
  "focalPoint": {
    "desktop": "70% 50%",
    "mobile": "50% 45%"
  },
  "alt": {
    "gu": "પસંદગીની ભારતીય મીઠાઈઓનું સુંદર આયોજન.",
    "en": "Assorted Indian sweets arranged for serving."
  }
}
```

Gujarati alt copy must be reviewed before launch.
